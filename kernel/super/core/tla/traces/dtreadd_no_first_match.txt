dtreadd_no_first_match -- NoErr (FIX_FIRST_MATCH off): "refcount underflow"

super_dev_lookup() returning the LAST (dev, sb) match instead of the first: s1 accepts d2
(entry 2) and releases it; its put takes entry 2 to zero, and before rhltable_remove() D1
re-registers (d2, s1): the lookup finds entry 2 dying, so a new entry 3 goes in front of
it (L1668-1672).  D1's unregister walks 3 -> 2 and keeps the last match, the dying entry
2: super_dev_put() at zero.  With the first match (L1647-1648) it finds 3: dtreadd_tree
passes.  Front insertion plus first match is what keeps a live entry ahead of every dying
one for the same (dev, sb).
