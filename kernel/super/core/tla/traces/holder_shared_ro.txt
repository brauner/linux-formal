holder_shared_ro -- FinalOK violated -- real (F2b on a shared erofs device): the emergency thaw leaves the shared device frozen and the next mount using it fails

Layout: two read-only (erofs) superblocks, s1 on d1 and s2 on d2, both with the extra
device d3 claimed with the file_system_type as holder (shared); dm3 suspends and
resumes d3; e is the emergency thaw.

 6-18  m2  s2 mounts with d3 ((d3, s2) published); s1's mount is slower.
 14-25 dm3 bdev_freeze(d3): the walk freezes s2 (the read-only shortcut).
 26-30 dm3 dm resume: bdev_thaw(d3) -> fs_bdev_thaw() pins (d3, s2), get_active_super().
 31-34 e   do_thaw_all_callback(s2): bdev_thaw(s2->s_bdev) = d2: -EINVAL;
           thaw_super_locked(FREEZE_HOLDER_USERSPACE) takes d3's hold.
 35-38 dm3 thaw_super(s2): -EINVAL; one superblock on d3 so far: returned;
           bd_fsfreeze_count(d3) stays 1.
 39-41 m1  s1 opens d3: fs_bdev_register() sees the count (L1670): -EBUSY, the mount
           of s1 fails.

erofs extra devices are never s_bdev, so any freeze of them is taken by the
emergency thaw's thaw_super_locked() rather than its bdev_thaw() loop.
holder_shared_ro_fixed passes with the proposed fs_bdev_thaw() change.
