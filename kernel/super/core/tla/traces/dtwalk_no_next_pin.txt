dtwalk_no_next_pin -- NoFreedReach (FIX_NEXT_PIN off)

super_dev_next() dropping prev before it reads prev->sd_node.next: C1's pin is the last
reference (s2 already dropped its claims), the put removes and frees the entry outside
any RCU section, and C1 would read ->next from freed memory.  The tree's order -- pin the
next one under RCU (L535-537), then super_dev_put(prev) (L539) -- is load-bearing.
