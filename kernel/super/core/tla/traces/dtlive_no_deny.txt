Without bdev_deny_freeze() around btrfs device add (btrfs_open_device_deny_freeze()):
 2,9   F2  bdev_freeze(d2) with no holder: count 1, no walk.
 3-14  s1  mounts on d1 and goes live (SB_BORN).
 15-20 s1  adds d2: opens it with fs_holder_ops, fs_bdev_register() inserts the entry (L486).
 21-25 F2  bdev_thaw(d2): s1's open set bd_holder_ops; the walk pins the new entry.
 26-27 F2  get_active_super(s1) succeeds at once (s1 is live); thaw_super(s1): never frozen,
           -EINVAL; count 1 -> bd_fsfreeze_count stays 1 for good (as dtdeg_tree).
(s1's registration then sees count 1 at L1678 and rolls back, too late.)  With the deny
(dtlive_tree) the add refuses while the device is frozen and a freeze refuses while the add
holds the deny.
