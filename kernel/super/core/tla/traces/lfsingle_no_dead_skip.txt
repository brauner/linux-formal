lfsingle_no_dead_skip -- NoErr (FIX_DEAD_SKIP off): "test() dereferences a freed s_fs_info"

Without sget_fc()'s SB_DEAD skip (L865) the scan runs test() on a superblock that
kill_anon_super() already marked dead and whose s_fs_info the filesystem freed after it
(m1's failed mount: SB_DYING, kill_super_notify(), free).  The skip is what lets
filesystems free s_fs_info after kill_anon_super() (2d2a2d7aa987).
