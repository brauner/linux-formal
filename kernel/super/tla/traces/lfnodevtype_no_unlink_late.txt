The shape before 2d2a2d7aa987: kill_super_notify() unlinked s_instances.  The iterator
holds a passive reference on S1, drops sb_lock for the callback, S1's teardown unlinks the
node, and the iterator retakes sb_lock and advances from an unlinked node (hlist_del_init()
leaves ->next NULL: the walk ends early and misses the rest).  Unlinking in put_super()
(L426) at the last passive reference keeps the iterator's node linked.
