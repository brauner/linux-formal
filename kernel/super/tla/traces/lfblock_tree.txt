lfblock_tree -- NoErr: "test() dereferences a freed s_fs_info"
(filesystem contract witness; every in-tree custom test() complies, see README)

Layout: a get_tree_single()-style filesystem whose test() dereferences s_fs_info and whose
->kill_sb() frees s_fs_info before deactivate_locked_super()'s kill_super_notify() (e.g.
generic_shutdown_super() or kill_block_super() and then kfree(s_fs_info)).

 2-10  m1  sget_fc(): no match, alloc_super(), set(), publish (L899-901), shrinker.
 11-12 m1  fill_super() fails: deactivate_locked_super() (s_umount held), s_active 0 (L584).
 13-14 m1  shrinker_free() (L585), ->kill_sb() -> generic_shutdown_super(): SB_DYING (L787),
           s_umount dropped (L788).
 15    m1  ->kill_sb() frees s_fs_info; SB_DEAD is not set yet (kill_super_notify() L588
           runs after ->kill_sb() returns).
 16-18 m2  sget_fc() under sb_lock: S1 is not SB_DEAD (L865), test() (L867) dereferences the
           freed s_fs_info.
kill_anon_super() (L1360-1365) runs kill_super_notify() right after
generic_shutdown_super(), SB_DEAD under sb_lock (L565-567), so a filesystem that frees
after kill_anon_super() returns is safe (lfsingle_tree).
