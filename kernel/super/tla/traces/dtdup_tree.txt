dtdup_tree -- NoErr: "passive reference of an entry dropped twice or never taken"
(caller contract witness: no in-tree caller registers the same (dev, sb) concurrently)

Layout: s1 with member d2 accepted and released before SB_BORN, and D1, a second task
registering and unregistering (d2, s1) at the same time.  Lines are fs/super.c at
fc1c25014ff8.

 11-16 D1, s1  both fs_bdev_register(d2, s1) look up (L1661) and find no (d2, s1) entry;
           super_dev_lookup() returns the first match only.
 17-20 s1  inserts entry 2 (L486), takes its passive reference (L488), passes L1678.
 22    s1  starts releasing its claim.
 24-25 D1  inserts entry 3 in front of entry 2 (rhltable_insert() puts duplicates first) --
           but has not run L488 yet.
 26-28 s1  fs_bdev_unregister(): super_dev_lookup() finds entry 3, D1's, first (L1772);
           super_dev_put() L1774: 1 -> 0, rhltable_remove(), put_super() -- the passive
           reference entry 3 never took (L471) -- and kfree_rcu().
           s_passive is now one too low; D1's refcount_inc() at L488 comes later, on an
           entry that is already unlinked and queued for freeing.

With both registrations complete the same race ends in the underflow: two live (d2, s1)
entries, the first unregister drops the front one to zero (claims are interchangeable),
and the second unregister's lookup (L1772, no reference taken) still finds that dying
entry first and super_dev_put()s it at zero: a refcount_t underflow warning, a
use-after-free once the grace period of its kfree_rcu() ended (the lookup's RCU section
closed at L1773), and the other entry -- with its passive reference on s1 -- is never put:
s1 is never freed.  Unregister picks the dying entry only when two live entries for the
same (dev, sb) existed, which needs two fs_bdev_register() calls for the same (dev, sb) in
flight at once; dtreadd_tree (the same pair, one after the other) passes.
