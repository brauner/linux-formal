wait_var_event_killable(&frozen, frozen == SB_UNFROZEN).  Stores to frozen are
buffered (WEAK_WAKE); wake_up_var() as at fc1c25014ff8 (no smp_mb()).

replaces, so the store-buffering window is real.  With the smp_mb() of the
worktree's sb_freeze_wake() (FIX_WAKE_MB, freeze_qwait_mb and freeze_qwait_ro_mb)
the wakeup is not lost.
