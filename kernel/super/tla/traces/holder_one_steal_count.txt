holder_one_steal_count -- CountOK violated -- real (F2a): after a FITHAW took dm's freeze, dm resume leaves bd_fsfreeze_count at 1 for good

Layout: as holder_one_steal.

 6-16  dm1 lock_fs() -> bdev_freeze(d1): count 1, s1 frozen through the walk.
 17-21 dm1 unlock_fs() (dm.c L3133, L2952) -> bdev_thaw() (block/bdev.c L341): count 1, so
           the holder's ->thaw (L359-361) -> fs_bdev_thaw() L1594, the walk pins
           (d1, s1), get_active_super().
 22    r1  FITHAW thaws s1 (as in holder_one_steal).
 23    dm1 fs_super_thaw() -> thaw_super(): not SB_FREEZE_COMPLETE -> -EINVAL (L2350-2351).
 26    dm1 one superblock: the error is returned (L1616-1618); bdev_thaw() decrements
           only without an error (block/bdev.c L367-368): count stays 1; unlock_fs()
           ignores the result and clears DMF_FROZEN (dm.c L2952-2953).

bd_fsfreeze_count is 1 with nobody holding a freeze, and nothing ever calls
bdev_thaw() for it again: after the umount the device can never be mounted
(setup_bdev_super() "Can't mount, blockdev is frozen", L1811-1815), btrfs refuses
to add/remove/replace it (bdev_deny_freeze() block/bdev.c L393-396), and every
later bdev_freeze() nests (block/bdev.c L311-314) without freezing the filesystem,
so the next dm snapshot is of a live filesystem.  Same end state as the bug
fe967191e585 fixed; its message's "Thawing without holding a freeze still fails
with -EINVAL" is not true while a block-device freeze holds freeze_ucount.
Reproducer: dmsetup suspend dut; fsfreeze -u /mnt; dmsetup resume dut; umount
/mnt; mount /dev/mapper/dut /mnt -> -EBUSY.  With fs_bdev_thaw() counting -EINVAL
as thawed (holder_one_steal_fixed) the count goes.
