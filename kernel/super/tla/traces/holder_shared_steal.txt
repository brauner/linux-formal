holder_shared_steal -- UserOK violated -- latent (F4): fs_bdev_thaw() thaws the superblock whose freeze failed, taking a FIFREEZE hold

Layout: as holder_shared_fail, plus f2 doing FIFREEZE on s2.

 15-30 dm3 bdev_freeze(d3): both per-superblock freezes fail, swallowed, returns 0.
 36    f2  FIFREEZE on s2: freeze_ucount 1, f2 holds it.
 31-37 dm3 bdev_thaw(d3) -> fs_bdev_thaw(): thaw_super(s2, FREEZE_MAY_NEST |
           FREEZE_HOLDER_USERSPACE) takes f2's hold (they share freeze_ucount): s2
           thawed while f2 believes it is frozen; f2's FITHAW will get -EINVAL.

Latent for the same reasons as holder_shared_fail (and erofs has no ->freeze_fs, so
FIFREEZE is -EOPNOTSUPP there, fs/ioctl.c L393-394).
