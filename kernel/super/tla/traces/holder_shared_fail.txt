holder_shared_fail -- FrozenWhileHeld violated -- documented (F4, 7a7ef107b449), latent: a swallowed per-superblock freeze error on a shared device

Layout: two read-write superblocks share d3 (in the tree only erofs shares, and erofs
is read-only); their freezes may fail.

 15-20 dm3 bdev_freeze(d3): the walk visits s1, freeze_super() fails (-EIO).
 22-26 dm3 s2's freeze fails as well.
 28    dm3 count = 2 > 1: the error is swallowed (L1576-1577); sync_blockdev().
 29-30 dm3 bdev_freeze() returns 0: the device counts as frozen, neither superblock is.

7a7ef107b449 chose this ("keep it frozen even if some of them failed to freeze and
swallow the error").  Unreachable in the tree: erofs superblocks are read-only, and
freeze_super()'s read-only shortcut (L2279-2287) cannot fail.
