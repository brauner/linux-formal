holder_multi_ethaw -- FinalOK violated -- real (F2b): the emergency thaw takes a freeze made through a device that is not s_bdev

Layout: s1 on d1 with the extra device d2 (an xfs log or rt device, an ext4 external
journal, a btrfs or f2fs member: opened with fs_bdev_file_open_by_path(), so
d2 has fs_holder_ops and an entry (d2, s1)); dm2 suspends and resumes d2; e is
the emergency thaw.

 2-12  m1  s1 mounts, registers (d2, s1) (L1664) and passes the check (L1670).
 11-24 dm2 bdev_freeze(d2) walks (d2, s1) and freezes s1: freeze_ucount 1.
 14,18 e   do_thaw_all_callback(s1): bdev_thaw(sb->s_bdev) -- d1, not frozen: -EINVAL,
           the loop (L1210-1212) never looks at d2.
 25-30 dm2 dm resume: bdev_thaw(d2) -> fs_bdev_thaw(), pins (d2, s1).
 29    e   thaw_super_locked(FREEZE_HOLDER_USERSPACE) (L1215) takes d2's hold, s1 thawed.
 31-34 dm2 fs_super_thaw(): -EINVAL; one superblock: returned; bd_fsfreeze_count(d2)
           stays 1 (block/bdev.c L367-368).

No race needed: a freeze through any device but s_bdev is always taken by
thaw_super_locked(), and that device's next bdev_thaw() fails.  The next mount of
the filesystem fails when it opens d2 (fs_bdev_register() -EBUSY, L1670-1673).
Since freezes propagate from every device (7366f8b6fc6a, v6.8).  holder_multi_ethaw_fixed
passes with the proposed fs_bdev_thaw() change.
