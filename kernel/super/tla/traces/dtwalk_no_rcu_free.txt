dtwalk_no_rcu_free -- NoFreedReach (FIX_RCU_FREE off)

super_dev_put() freeing with kfree() at removal: cursor C1 reads d2's head (entry 1,
L525-526) inside its RCU section, s2's kill_super_notify() drops the last claim (L553),
removes and frees the entry; C1 is still about to read it.  kfree_rcu() (L472) is
load-bearing.
