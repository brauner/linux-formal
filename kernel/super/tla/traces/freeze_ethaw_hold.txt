freeze_ethaw_hold -- HoldsOK violated -- documented: the emergency thaw takes block-device holds

Layout: b freezes through a block device; E is the emergency thaw.

 2-14  b  a full freeze through fs_super_freeze(): freeze_ucount 1.
 6,15  E  do_thaw_all_callback() L1204: get_active_super(), super_lock_excl() +
          thaw_super_locked(FREEZE_HOLDER_USERSPACE) L1214-1215: takes b's hold and
          thaws.

That is what sysrq-j is for.  The consequence for the block device (its next
bdev_thaw() fails and bd_fsfreeze_count stays) is SuperHolder's holder_multi_ethaw
and holder_one_ethaw.
